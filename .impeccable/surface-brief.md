# V2.5 approved homepage direction

MODE: Experience with a clear enquiry path for businesses and independent professionals in La Ciotat and its surroundings.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One continuous exhibition introduces the work and leads to the visitor's own project. Exactly SpoolIQ, Loprim3D and Belofer; no historical gallery or process section.

OWN-WORLD: Light Tech, locally hosted DM Sans, warm white, near-black and lime. C's project navigation and changing image frames combine with B's strong, offset sans headline. Authentic images remain the primary evidence.

STORY: Understand the service and location, see the application, explore the store and archived business website, recognize three useful capabilities, meet Ricardo briefly and send an enquiry through the full existing form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At390px, compact accessible header, bold two-line commercial headline, concise service/locality and contact CTA lead directly into the project navigation and large SpoolIQ image. The first project owns the image; it is not repeated by the hero. Desktop gives the title expressive scale and an offset second line, then progresses that same image into the pinned exhibition.

FORM: Owner-selected Concept C with Concept B typography, approved in the V2.5 final implementation brief. Existing code-led prototypes and their browser captures are the references; no additional concept roll or selection is requested or needed. Mobile native flow and keyboard/reduced-motion access outrank rigid spatial imitation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Production code stays in the existing Next.js app. Preserve all locales, SEO, original project records/assets and Netlify form contracts. No new dependencies, merge, push or deploy. Original raster provenance remains in docs/SOURCES.md.
